PROJECT NAME:
"SevaSetu" – Multi-NGO Education, Donation & Community Support Platform

DOMAIN:
Use a configurable domain such as abcyz.in

OBJECTIVE:
Build a complete, production-ready Web + Mobile Responsive application that allows multiple NGOs to work together to support poor and underprivileged children and families.

The platform must support:
1. Multiple NGOs
2. Poor/underprivileged children
3. Students and parents/guardians
4. Education programs
5. School/scholarship management
6. Donations
7. Clothing distribution
8. Food distribution
9. Books and stationery
10. Inventory
11. Volunteers
12. Donor management
13. Beneficiary management
14. Events
15. Fundraising campaigns
16. Sponsorship
17. Admin management
18. NGO-specific administration
19. Reports and analytics
20. Documents and verification
21. Notifications
22. Audit logs
23. Website CMS
24. Mobile-friendly PWA
25. Multi-language support
26. Future integration with payment gateways, SMS, WhatsApp and email.

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT create a simple demo.
Build the application architecture as a real production-ready platform.

Every button, menu, tab, form, filter, search box, dashboard card and workflow must work.
