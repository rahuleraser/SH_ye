# SevaSetu User Manual

## Public visitor

Browse NGOs, campaigns, events and impact. Donate without an account. Apply to volunteer or request support.

## Donor

Register or donate as guest. Receipts are generated with `RCPT-` numbers. Recurring frequency is stored for finance follow-up.

## NGO admin

`/ngo` — manage beneficiaries, students, inventory, warehouses, distributions, volunteers, scholarships, expenses, vendors, assets, documents, reports and CSV import.

Inventory: Stock IN / Stock OUT updates quantities and writes a transaction. Low-stock creates a notification.

Distribution teams can "Save offline" on a field device and "Sync queue" when connectivity returns.

## Teacher

Classes, attendance, assignments, exams and results.

## Super admin

`/admin` — approve NGOs, users, CMS, settings, backups, platform-wide reports and audit logs.
